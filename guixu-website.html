Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题

Quick answers for the curious.

快速解答大家关心的话题。

Do I need a powerful computer? 我需要高性能电脑吗？ ▼

No. Any device with internet connection and basic computing capability can join — from an old laptop to a Raspberry Pi. The more stable and resource-rich your device, the higher your contribution score, but even modest devices are welcome.

不需要。任何能联网且具备基本计算能力的设备都可以加入 — 从旧笔记本到树莓派。设备越稳定、资源越丰富，贡献值越高，但即使配置一般的设备也能参与。

What exactly do I contribute? 我具体贡献什么？ ▼

Your device contributes its idle resources — bandwidth for node communication, storage for the distributed ledger, and compute power for consensus verification. The client monitors usage but never affects your daily tasks.

你的设备贡献闲置资源 — 带宽用于节点间通信，存储用于分布式账本，计算能力用于共识验证。客户端监控使用量但不会影响你的日常使用。

How are tokens distributed? 代币如何分配？ ▼

There is no private or public sale. All tokens are minted on demand based on terminal resource contribution. The device that contributes more gets more. It's a fair system: contribution determines reward.

没有私募或公募。所有代币都按需铸造，取决于终端资源贡献。贡献多的获得更多，公平而透明：贡献即奖励。

Is Guixu a scam? How do I trust it? 归墟是骗局吗？如何信任它？ ▼

Guixu has no VC funding, no insiders, no pre-mine. The code is open, the rules are transparent on-chain, and the maintenance fund is managed by community vote. We invite you to verify everything yourself.

归墟没有风投，没有内幕，没有提前挖。代码开源，规则链上透明，运维基金由社区投票管理。我们邀请你亲自验证一切。

Can I use this alongside my regular computer use? 我可以和日常使用电脑一起运行吗？ ▼

Absolutely. The client only uses truly idle resources. If your device is busy with tasks, the client scales down. It's designed to work quietly in the background without impacting your experience.

完全可以。客户端只使用真正闲置的资源。如果你的设备正在忙，客户端会自动降频。它设计为在后台安静运行，不影响你的正常使用体验。
