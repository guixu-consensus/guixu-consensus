Chapter 1: Project Background & Vision

In 2026, compute power remains concentrated in mining farms, and nodes still operate silently within server clusters. The idle computers, mobile phones, bandwidth caps, and storage of ordinary people have never been integrated into blockchain's consensus networks. The centralization of compute and network makes the rhetoric of decentralization seem hollow in reality.

The Guixu Consensus Network is born from a simple belief: true decentralization should not require anyone to sacrifice their own devices. Idle resources in everyone's hands are, in themselves, the consensus power that blockchain most desperately needs.

Naming Meaning

Guixu (The Great Abyss)

Guixu is a concept from ancient Chinese mythology, first appearing in "Liezi · Tang Wen" (The Questions of Tang). It refers to the bottomless abyss where all ocean currents converge, where every river eventually flows into, yet its volume never increases.

English Definition: Guixu (The Great Abyss) / Oceanic Abyss / The Great Sinkhole

Symbolism: The oceanic abyss's theme of "all things converging to one" and "never depleting" symbolizes terminal devices from across the world pooling together to form a unified consensus network. Resources are continuously utilized and perpetuate. Just as rivers and streams all eventually reach the sea, every person with idle devices is a participant in building a decentralized world.

Core Philosophy

"Resources are compute, contribution is consensus."
No expensive mining rigs and server clusters are needed. Every idle computer, every idle phone, as long as it stays online, contributes bandwidth, provides storage, and runs stably, is a valid node in the blockchain consensus network.

Project Vision

To build a blockchain consensus system truly based on distributed terminal resources, allowing every person with idle devices to participate equally in the construction of a blockchain network and earn returns. Zero threshold, no private sale, no public sale, fully decentralized.

Through standardized interfaces they connect directly to real-world demand-side platforms, forming a complete commercial loop: contribute → aggregate → match → monetize → reinvest. This is Guixu's core survival capability and what distinguishes it from purely conceptual networks — its resources always have real buyers.

Chapter 3: Token Economic Model

Token Basic Information

The Guixu Consensus Network token (hereinafter referred to as the "Guixu Token") is the sole credential for community members to participate in consensus network construction and earn contribution rewards. This token continuously provides funding for the operations reserve fund through the contract's built-in "transaction tax" and "transfer tax" mechanisms.

Token Issuance Rules

Item Rule
Total SupplyMinted on demand, no fixed upper limit, issuance speed determined dynamically by contribution scores
Issuance MethodNo private sale, no public sale, all minted automatically through terminal resource contribution
Distribution MechanismAllocated proportionally by terminal resource contribution score: more contribution equals more rewards
Minting Trigger ConditionAutomatic minting and allocation when terminal contribution reaches a certain threshold
Minting CooldownEach mint separated by 1-hour interval to prevent frequent small minting

Tax Mechanism — Operations Reserve Fund

Transaction Tax: Each transaction (Transfer) incurs a 1% Guixu token transaction tax.
Transfer Tax: Each transfer incurs a 0.5% Guixu token transfer tax.
Anti-Bot Strategy: The tax rates are deliberately set high, substantially increasing the cost for automated scripts to batch-farm and exploit the system, protecting the interests of genuine token holders.
Rate Rationale: Tax rates can be adjusted via community governance voting, ensuring fees both provide sufficient operations funding and do not excessively impact user experience.
Fund Destination: All tax revenue automatically flows into the operations reserve pool, used for node reward distribution, network maintenance, security audits, and system upgrades.

Contribution Score Calculation Model

Contribution Score = Σ(Resource Type × Contribution Coefficient × Contribution Duration × Stability Weight × Anti-Exploit Coefficient)

Consensus Algorithm — PoR (Proof of Resource)

Traditional PoW consumes massive electricity; PoS depends on locked capital. The Guixu Consensus Network adopts the PoR (Proof of Resource) consensus mechanism: it replaces capital staking and hashrate competition with resource contribution, establishing an independent path distinct from PoW and PoS; the technical implementation draws on PoS's stake-weighting approach, but the weight derives from resource contribution rather than token staking.

  • Terminals prove their contribution value through continuous contribution of idle resources (bandwidth, storage, compute)
  • The system real-time calculates each terminal's contribution score ranking
  • Terminals with the highest contribution scores gain priority in token minting rights
  • All resource contribution records are stored on-chain, immutable

Chapter 5: Operations & Governance

Community Governance

The system operates on fully decentralized governance. All major decisions are determined by Guixu token holder voting:

  • Tax rate adjustments (modification of 1% transaction tax and 0.5% transfer tax)
  • Contribution coefficient adjustments (change of weights for each resource type)
  • Operations reserve fund usage approval
  • Technology upgrades and protocol changes
  • Malicious node punishment

Operations Reserve Fund

Funding Source: Transaction tax (1%) + Transfer tax (0.5%), flowing continuously.
Fund Usage:
  • Node reward distribution (40%)
  • Network maintenance and security audits (30%)
  • System upgrades and development (20%)
  • Community building and marketing (10%)
Fund Oversight: All fund flows are fully transparent on-chain and auditable. Periodic fund reports are published.

We believe that true decentralization is not a slogan, but practice. No expensive mining rigs, no massive capital thresholds, just your idle devices, your idle bandwidth, and your idle storage. These resources are your chips, your contribution, your vote for a decentralized world.
