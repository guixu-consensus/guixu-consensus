Opening Note · Platform Positioning & Value Statement

I. Platform Positioning: A Resource Value Network

The Guixu Consensus Network is not merely a DApp or a tool, but a resource value network — its core mission is to aggregate the physical resources and traffic resources scattered across users' idle devices and idle attention, connect them to real commercial demand, and form a sustainable commercial loop.

II. Dual-Layer Resource Model

Physical Resources: Users' idle devices (PCs, phones, routers, edge nodes, etc.) are inherently capable of computing, storing and transmitting. Within the Guixu network these capabilities are scheduled as compute nodes, storage nodes, bandwidth nodes and edge-computing nodes. The value of physical resources has no ceiling — the more devices, and the more idle they are, the larger the physical foundation the platform can mobilize.

Traffic Resources: Users' attention, visit behavior, social relations and content-consumption paths are all traffic. Through the terminal entry (node.guixu.club), desktop and mobile, Guixu builds multi-touch reach and turns users' active behavior into a measurable traffic pool. The value of traffic resources likewise has no ceiling — the larger the user base, the stronger the stickiness and the richer the content ecosystem, the greater the commercial pricing room of the traffic pool.

III. Token Economy as the Value Anchor

The token is not the goal, but the carrier of value consensus. Guixu operates in three phases:

  • Phase 1 · Token Traffic-Building, Building Consensus: Use the token economy as a hook — participate to earn, contribute to be rewarded, hold to benefit — to attract early users. Here the token lowers the cost of trust: users need not understand complex underlying technology, they only need to know "I participate, I benefit".
  • Phase 2 · Token Supports Business, Converging Value: Once user scale reaches a critical point, the token begins to play a business-anchoring role — contributors of physical resources earn token rewards, forming sustained participation incentives; producers of traffic resources (terminal users) receive token returns through their behavior; commercial demand-side parties purchase platform resources through the token system. The token becomes the universal yardstick of resource value, letting physical and traffic resources be priced and traded under one valuation system.
  • Phase 3 · Token Steps Back, the Commercial Loop Runs Independently: As the platform matures, the token shifts from "attractor" to "settler". The commercial loop no longer treats the token itself as the goal; instead the token serves as the medium of value flow, ensuring every participant's contribution is fairly rewarded.

IV. Commercial Loop

Idle devices + user attention → Guixu terminal (entry layer) → Physical resources converge (compute/storage/bandwidth) + traffic resources converge (visits/behavior/social) → Token incentives + token behavior rewards → Guixu Consensus Network (value hub) → Demand side (enterprises/developers/projects) → Resource monetization (physical resource sales) + traffic monetization (ads/promotion/distribution) → Token distribution (reward contributors) → Users keep contributing, forming a positive cycle

The key to this loop is sustainability — physical resources do not shrink through consumption (on the contrary, the more they are used, the more mature the devices and the larger the network); traffic resources do not dry up through monetization (users' commercial behavior can feed back to users, generating more contribution). The token acts as lubricant, ensuring value flows fairly among contributors, platform and demand side.

Idle resources aggregated by the Guixu Consensus Network are not merely bookkept internally. Through standardized interfaces they connect directly to real-world demand-side platforms, forming a complete commercial loop: contribute → aggregate → match → monetize → reinvest. This is Guixu's core survival capability and what distinguishes it from purely conceptual networks — its resources always have real buyers.

Beyond direct monetization of the three physical resource classes, the growth of the network itself constitutes independent commercial value. As connected terminals and active addresses keep increasing, the traffic, user attention and transaction activity accumulated by the platform can themselves form token economic value: the transaction tax continuously feeds the Operations Reserve Fund, rising network activity drives token demand, and ultimately a positive three-way loop emerges among resource contributors, demand-side buyers and token holders.

V. Value Statement Summary

The value of the Guixu Consensus Network is not single-dimensional. It is a complete system that starts from idle user resources, takes the token as the cornerstone of consensus, resource aggregation as the means, and the commercial loop as the goal.

  • For users: turn idle resources into income, attention into value, participation into rights.
  • For business: use the token as a credit medium and the platform as a credit carrier to obtain precise, measurable and sustainable traffic and compute resources.
  • For the platform: neither sells nor hoards resources, but builds a value infrastructure where resources converge, price and flow naturally.

The endgame of business is not extraction, but a continuing cycle of mutual benefit. What Guixu wants to do is to let every contribution, every visit and every transaction find its place in this cycle.

Chapter 1: Project Background & Vision

In 2026, compute power remains concentrated in mining farms, and nodes still operate silently within server clusters. The idle computers, mobile phones, bandwidth caps, and storage of ordinary people have never been integrated into blockchain's consensus networks. The centralization of compute and network makes the rhetoric of decentralization seem hollow in reality.

The Guixu Consensus Network is born from a simple belief: true decentralization should not require anyone to sacrifice their own devices. Idle resources in everyone's hands are, in themselves, the consensus power that blockchain most desperately needs.

Naming Meaning

Guixu (The Great Abyss)

Guixu is a concept from ancient Chinese mythology, first appearing in "Liezi · Tang Wen" (The Questions of Tang). It refers to the bottomless abyss where all ocean currents converge, where every river eventually flows into, yet its volume never increases.

English Definition: Guixu (The Great Abyss) / Oceanic Abyss / The Great Sinkhole

Symbolism: The oceanic abyss's theme of "all things converging to one" and "never depleting" symbolizes terminal devices from across the world pooling together to form a unified consensus network. Resources are continuously utilized and perpetuate. Just as rivers and streams all eventually reach the sea, every person with idle devices is a participant in building a decentralized world.

Core Philosophy

"Resources are compute, contribution is consensus."
No expensive mining rigs and server clusters are needed. Every idle computer, every idle phone, as long as it stays online, contributes bandwidth, provides storage, and runs stably, is a valid node in the blockchain consensus network.

Project Vision

To build a blockchain consensus system truly based on distributed terminal resources, allowing every person with idle devices to participate equally in the construction of a blockchain network and earn returns. Zero threshold, no private sale, no public sale, fully decentralized.

Chapter 3: Token Economic Model

Token Basic Information

The Guixu Consensus Network token (hereinafter referred to as "gx") is the sole credential for community members to participate in consensus network construction and earn contribution rewards. This token continuously provides funding for the operations reserve fund through the contract's built-in "transaction tax" mechanism.

Token Issuance Rules

Item Rule
Total SupplyMinted on demand, no fixed upper limit, issuance speed determined dynamically by contribution scores
Issuance MethodNo private sale, no public sale, all minted automatically through terminal resource contribution
Distribution MechanismAllocated proportionally by terminal resource contribution score: more contribution equals more rewards
Minting Trigger ConditionAutomatic minting and allocation when terminal contribution reaches a certain threshold
Minting CooldownEach mint separated by 1-hour interval to prevent frequent small minting

Tax Mechanism — Operations Reserve Fund

Transaction Tax: Each transaction (Transfer) incurs a 1% gx transaction tax.
Anti-Bot Strategy: The tax rates are deliberately set high, substantially increasing the cost for automated scripts to batch-farm and exploit the system, protecting the interests of genuine token holders.
Rate Rationale: Tax rates can be adjusted via community governance voting, ensuring fees both provide sufficient operations funding and do not excessively impact user experience.
Fund Destination: All tax revenue automatically flows into the operations reserve pool, used for node reward distribution, network maintenance, security audits, and system upgrades.

Contribution Score Calculation Model

Contribution Score = Σ(Resource Type × Contribution Coefficient × Contribution Duration × Stability Weight × Anti-Exploit Coefficient)

Consensus Algorithm — PoR (Proof of Resource)

Traditional PoW consumes massive electricity; PoS depends on locked capital. The Guixu Consensus Network adopts the PoR (Proof of Resource) consensus mechanism: it replaces capital staking and hashrate competition with resource contribution, establishing an independent path distinct from PoW and PoS; the technical implementation draws on PoS's stake-weighting approach, but the weight derives from resource contribution rather than token staking.

  • Terminals prove their contribution value through continuous contribution of idle resources (bandwidth, storage, compute)
  • The system real-time calculates each terminal's contribution score ranking
  • Terminals with the highest contribution scores gain priority in token minting rights
  • All resource contribution records are stored on-chain, immutable

Chapter 9: Operating Norms for the Resource Value Network

The Guixu Consensus Network relies not only on the aggregation of physical resources, but also on precise definitions of "people" and "behavior." The following three role definitions are the core prerequisites for the sustainable operation of the network's commercial loop.

I. Definition of Effective Traffic User

Guixu reaches users through multiple touchpoints — the terminal entrance (node.guixu.club), desktop, and mobile — transforming user activity into a measurable traffic pool. However, traffic resources must exclude bots and automated farming; otherwise the commercial monetization end (advertising / promotion / distribution) loses its pricing power.

An access event counts as effective traffic only if it simultaneously meets all of the following conditions:

DimensionStandardImplementation
Identity UniquenessOne wallet address per natural person, bound to a device fingerprintWallet signature + device fingerprint hash (IP + User-Agent + screen resolution + Canvas fingerprint); new wallets on different devices require behavioral verification
Non-AutomatedBehavior has randomness, dwell time and interaction; no fixed script patternBehavior analysis engine: records mouse trajectory, scroll speed, key intervals; deviations from human distributions (e.g. constant-speed scrolling, fixed-interval clicks) trigger automatic downweighting
Effective DwellSingle-session dwell ≥ 15 seconds with at least 1 meaningful interaction (click, form input, route jump)Frontend tracking + backend aggregation; the 15s threshold is adjustable via community governance

Fraud Prevention Tiers:

L2 (Human + Assistive Script): human operation assisted by automation tools → behavior deviates from human distribution, coefficient drops to 0.3

III. Definition of Market Promoter (Team Lead)

The Guixu Consensus Network has no private placement and no public offering, yet it requires market promotion. A team lead must not operate as a "recruit-heads-and-take-commission" pyramid scheme, nor "sit back and earn" in a way that undermines PoR consensus decentralization. Promotion incentives are realized through "contribution-value bonuses + team activity rewards," not "direct token dividends."

A role that simultaneously satisfies the following criteria is defined as an effective team lead:

DimensionCriterionNote
Recruitment ScaleDirectly invited ≥ 10 effective resource contributors (Lv1 or above) to join the Guixu NetworkOnly effective contributors count; invalid traffic / zero-contribution registrations excluded
Team Quality≥ 30% of team members are Lv2 Premium ContributorsPrevents padding with low-spec devices
Personal ParticipationThe team lead must also be an effective resource contributor (Lv1 or above)Avoids pure recruitment models
Continuous ActivityTeam average daily active terminals ≥ 15 over the past 30 daysPrevents dissolution after one-off recruitment

Incentive Structure (three tiers, progressively unlocked):

Tier 1 · Recruitment Reward (one-time): each Lv1 effective contributor recruited → +5% contribution bonus; each Lv2 → +15%; each Lv3 core contributor → +30%. The bonus applies only to the team lead's personal contribution score and does not affect overall network issuance speed.

Tier 2 · Team Activity Reward (ongoing): team average daily active terminals ≥ 20 → an additional 0.5% of total team contribution value (paid from the 10% community-promotion budget of the operations reserve); ≥ 50 → raised to 1.0%. Paid monthly and not counted toward token issuance.

Tier 3 · Ecosystem Contribution Reward (long-term): when resources contributed by team members are purchased by commercial demand parties, the team lead earns 1% of the purchase amount (paid from commercialization revenue and not counted toward token issuance); applies only to purchases generated by Lv2+ members.

Core Logic Behind the Three Roles:

  • Effective Traffic User — ensures the traffic pool consists of real humans, preserving pricing power for commercial monetization
  • Effective Resource Contributor — ensures the physical resource pool meets quality standards, making commercial demand-side purchasing viable
  • Market Promoter — replaces direct dividends with contribution-value bonuses, preserving PoR consensus fairness while incentivizing community growth
