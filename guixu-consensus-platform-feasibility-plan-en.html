Chapter 1: Project Background & Operations Status

1.1 Project Background

In 2026, the blockchain industry is facing three major bottlenecks: compute centralization, high barriers to entry, and energy waste. The compute power of existing public chains is monopolized by professional mining farms, PoW mining consumes massive electricity, and PoS requires large token stakes—none of which align with the original intent of decentralization.

The Guixu Consensus Platform proposes a brand-new approach: true decentralization does not require users to invest in purchasing mining rigs, but instead allows every person with idle devices to participate in the consensus network. Idle computers, phones, routers, and NAS—these resources themselves are the consensus power that blockchain most desperately needs.

1.2 Project Positioning

Project Nature: Decentralized consensus network platform
Core Mechanism: PoR (Proof of Resource) Resource Proof Consensus
Token Type: gx
Operations Mode: Community Co-Governance, on-chain governance, fully transparent on-chain
Funding Model: 100% decentralized, no reliance on external financing, self-sufficient through the transaction tax

The project is currently in the foundational concept phase, with whitepaper completed in Chinese and English. PoR algorithm prototype validation and beta testing are planned for Q1-Q2 2027, mainnet launch for Q3, and open phase for Q4.

Chapter 3: Community Governance Mechanism

3.1 PoR Consensus Mechanism

The Guixu Consensus Platform adopts the PoR (Proof of Resource) consensus mechanism: it replaces capital staking and hashrate competition with resource contribution, establishing an independent path distinct from PoW and PoS; the technical implementation draws on PoS's stake-weighting approach, but the weight derives from resource contribution rather than token staking.

Idle Terminals → Contribute Resources → Calculate Score → Rank & Mint
  • Terminals prove their contribution value through continuous contribution of idle resources (bandwidth, storage, compute)
  • The system real-time calculates each terminal's contribution score ranking
  • Terminals with the highest contribution scores gain priority in token minting rights
  • All resource contribution records are stored on-chain, immutable

3.3 Tax Mechanism — Operations Reserve Fund

Transaction Tax: 1% gx per transaction (Transfer)
Rate Adjustment: Tax rates can be adjusted via community governance voting
Fund Destination: Transaction tax revenue automatically flows into the operations reserve pool

Fund Usage Allocation: Node rewards (40%), network maintenance & security audits (30%), system upgrades & development (20%), community building & marketing (10%)

3.4 Community Governance Voting

gx holders participate in community governance through on-chain voting. Key voting items include:

  • Tax rate adjustments (modification of the 1% transaction tax)
  • Contribution coefficient adjustments (changes to weights for each resource type)
  • Operations reserve fund usage approval
  • Technology upgrades and protocol changes
  • Malicious node punishment

Market Promoter (Team Lead) definition (aligned with Whitepaper Chapter 9, Section 2):
The model uses "contribution bonus + team activity reward" rather than direct token profit-sharing. A valid Team Lead must simultaneously: have directly invited ≥ 10 Effective Resource Contributors (Lv1+), have ≥ 30% of the team at Lv2, be an Lv1+ contributor personally, and maintain a team daily-active average of ≥ 15 terminals over the past 30 days.
Three incentive tiers: (1) Recruitment Reward (one-off) — each Lv1 / Lv2 / Lv3 recruited grants a +5% / +15% / +30% contribution bonus, applied to the Team Lead's own score only and not affecting network-wide issuance speed; (2) Team Activity Reward — 0.5% of total team contribution at ≥ 20 daily-active terminals, rising to 1.0% at ≥ 50, paid monthly from the Operations Reserve Fund's community budget and excluded from token issuance; (3) Ecosystem Contribution Reward — 1% of purchase value when team members' resources are commercially procured, paid from commercial revenue and valid only for Lv2+ members.
Full definition and anti-fraud mechanisms: Whitepaper Chapter 9, Section 2.

Chapter 5: Commercial Monetization & Resource Matching

5.1 Overall Commercial Approach

For the project to survive long term, "idle resources" must generate real sales revenue. The Guixu Consensus Platform is not a closed mining network but a resource aggregation and scheduling platform: it standardizes the storage, bandwidth and compute scattered across users' devices and matches them to professional platforms with genuine purchasing demand.

In one sentence: the consensus layer meters resources → the business layer sells them → revenue flows back to participants and the operations fund → more devices join, forming a self-sustaining commercial loop.

5.3 Converting Traffic into Token Economy Value

Once the network has accumulated enough devices and users, that traffic itself carries commercial value. The platform can match this traffic with partnership demand to generate revenue, and route that revenue back to network participants through resource settlement and token mechanisms — giving the token real usage demand rather than issuance supply alone.

Idle resources → aggregation & metering by Guixu → purchased by professional demand side → revenue returns → participant payouts + operations fund → network scale expands
Effective Traffic User definition (aligned with Whitepaper Chapter 9, Section 1):
Traffic can only be monetized if it can be priced, and it can only be priced if it is genuinely human. An access event counts as effective traffic only if it meets all four: identity uniqueness (one wallet per natural person, bound to a device fingerprint), non-automated behaviour (randomness, dwell time and interaction; no fixed script pattern), meaningful dwell (single session ≥ 15 seconds with at least one meaningful interaction), and source quality (excluding crawlers, CDN prefetch and known proxy pools).
Anti-fraud tiers: L1 automated scripts are blocked outright and generate no contribution value; L2 human-plus-script-assisted is reduced to a 0.3 coefficient; L3 fully human starts at 1.0 and gradually rises to 1.5 after 30 consecutive days of effective online presence.
Full definition: Whitepaper Chapter 9, Section 1.

5.4 Revenue Structure & Resilience

  • No single-source dependency: storage, bandwidth, compute and traffic advance in parallel; any one of them maturing is enough to sustain basic network operations.
  • Asset-light cost structure: resources come from users' idle devices at near-zero marginal cost, giving a price advantage over traditional IDC and compute markets.
  • Professional buyers: purchasers are established storage, bandwidth and compute platforms with relatively rigid demand and the ability to sign long-term agreements.
  • Compliant and controllable: resources are delivered as standardized services without capital raising, and combined with community governance and the operations fund mechanism to reduce legal and operational risk.
