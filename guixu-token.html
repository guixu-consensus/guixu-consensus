Tax 1% buy / 1% sell: 50% to the on-chain dividend vault, 30% to the liquidity pool, 10% to the creator, 10% burned. Independent of the Guixu mainnet token. 部署于 BNB Chain，经 Flap 公开联合曲线发行的社区代币。公平启动，零预挖、无预售、无预留额度。买入税、卖出税各 1%：五成进入链上分红金库，三成注入流动性池，一成分配给创作者，一成用于销毁。与归墟主网代币相互独立。

01 — Mechanism

How the curve works曲线机制

Price is discovered on-chain between reserve and circulating supply. There is no pre-built liquidity pool and no manual price setting at this stage. 价格由储备与流通量在链上共同决定，曲线阶段不存在预建流动性池，也不存在人工挂单定价。

01

Bonding curve联合曲线

Buying releases tokens from the curve and grows the reserve; selling returns reserve to the seller and shrinks it. Supply drives price, not order books. 买入时合约释放代币、储备增加；卖出时回收代币、储备减少。价格由储备与流通共同决定，而非订单簿。

02

Graduation毕业迁移

At 80% of total supply in circulation (800M tokens, approx. 16 BNB reserve), remaining supply and the full reserve migrate to PancakeSwap. Tax tokens migrate to a V2 pool. 流通量达到总量 80%（8 亿枚，约 16 BNB 储备）时，剩余代币与全部储备自动迁移至 PancakeSwap；税币迁移目标为 V2 池。

03

Tax routing税收路由

Trade tax is fixed at deployment and cannot be changed afterwards: 1% on buys, 1% on sells. Of that, 50% funds the on-chain dividend vault, 30% is added to the liquidity pool, 10% goes to the creator and 10% is burned. Nothing is routed outside those four buckets. 交易税在创建时写死、之后不可更改：买入 1%、卖出 1%。其中五成进入链上分红金库，三成注入流动性池，一成分配给创作者，一成用于销毁；四项之外不流向任何钱包。

03 — Boundary

Boundary with the Guixu mainnet与归墟主网的边界

This asset is a separate community object. The statements below are commitments, not marketing language, and this page is the only authoritative statement of the relationship between this asset and the Guixu mainnet. 本资产是独立的社区物件。以下为承诺性表述，不作宣传包装；本页是本资产与归墟主网关系的唯一权威表述。

  • Independent asset.独立资产。 It is not the Guixu mainnet token and carries no mainnet emission rights.它不是归墟主网代币，不承载任何主网发行权益。
  • No impact on the roadmap.不影响路线图。 PoR consensus, the node program and the 2027+ roadmap are unaffected by this launch.PoR 共识、节点程序与 2027 起的路线图不受本次发射影响。
  • Two different taxes.两种税不是一回事。 Trade tax applies to this token only. It is unrelated to the mainnet transaction fee.交易税仅作用于本代币，与主网交易税无关，二者不可混同。
  • Optional identity, not entitlement.可选身份，非服务权益。 Holding is an optional identity layer. It grants no access right, no service and no return.持币仅为可选身份层，不构成任何访问权、服务权益或回报。
  • No mapping, no conversion, no distribution.不映射、不兑换、不空投。 This asset does not map to the mainnet token, cannot be converted into it, and receives no share of any mainnet revenue or emission.本资产不映射主网代币、不可兑换为主网代币，也不分取主网的任何收益或发行份额。
  • No governance or node rights.不授予治理与节点权益。 Holding grants no governance power over the mainnet, no node qualification and no claim on the resource marketplace.持币不授予对主网的治理权、不构成节点资格，也不对资源市场提出任何主张。
  • Documentation boundary.文档边界。 The mainnet whitepaper does not describe, include or endorse this asset; nothing in the whitepaper applies to it, and this page is not part of the whitepaper.归墟主网白皮书不描述、不包含、也不背书本资产；白皮书中任何表述均不适用于本资产，本页不属于白皮书体系。

  • Q1 · What is this asset?Q1 · 这是什么资产？ A community asset on BNB Smart Chain, issued through a public bonding curve on Flap. It is not the Guixu mainnet token and carries no rights over it.一个部署在 BNB Smart Chain、通过 Flap 公开联合曲线发行的社区资产。它不是归墟主网代币，也不对主网主张任何权利。
