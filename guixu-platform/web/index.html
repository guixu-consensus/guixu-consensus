Resource matching · the commercial loop

The platform does not rely on token-price narratives — resources connect directly to real demand-side businesses, forming sustainable commercial viability

01 / STORAGE

Disk storage

Fragmented idle space is aggregated into a large-scale cold-data pool, providing replica redundancy and long-term archiving.

→ Matching: professional storage providers
02 / BANDWIDTH

Uplink bandwidth

Idle uplink spread across regions and carriers is a natural fit for edge-distribution scenarios.

→ Matching: CDN / edge computing / mining operators
03 / COMPUTE

AI compute

Idle GPU and CPU capacity joins the scheduling pool to serve batch tasks such as model inference and rendering.

→ Matching: AI training and inference providers
04 / TOKEN

Traffic token economy

The traffic and trading activity accumulated by the platform itself creates a token-economy effect that continuously feeds the operations fund.

→ Source: 1% transaction tax + 0.5% transfer tax
